Common questions

I forgot my password. On the sign-in screen choose "Forgot password", enter your email and follow the link we send you.

How do I delete my account and data? In the app go to Settings → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and everything in it.

Can I change my wheel later? Yes. Settings → Edit your wheel. You choose which week the new version starts from, and your past weeks keep their history.

Can I fill in a week I missed? Yes. Open the Log tab and choose any past week.
