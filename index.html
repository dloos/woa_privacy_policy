Wheel of Alignment — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 1 August 2026

Wheel of Alignment ("the app", "we", "us") is a personal reflection and time-alignment app. This page explains what information the app collects, why, and how it is stored. We built this app to help a small group of people reflect on how they spend their time — we do not sell data, run ads, or share your information with advertisers or analytics companies.

Who runs this app

Wheel of Alignment is an independently developed app. If you have questions about this policy or your data, contact tech@samanthatomkinson.com.

What information we collect

All data below is tied to your account and stored using Supabase, our backend provider. We do not use any analytics, advertising, or crash-reporting services.

DataWhy we collect it
Email addressUsed to create and secure your account, and to send you sign-in and password-reset emails.
Display nameShown in the app so it feels personal to you.
PasswordStored securely (hashed) by our authentication provider to protect your account. We never see or store your password in plain text.
Profile details you choose to addOptional fields like age range, gender, country, timezone, work status, and whether you have dependents — used only to personalize your experience within the app.
Wheel, category, and campaign dataThe categories, hours, and commitments you set up to track your own time and priorities.
Weekly logs and reflectionsYour logged hours, alignment scores, weekly notes, and reflective writing (e.g. purpose statements) — this is the core content of the app and is private to your account.
Sleep hoursSelf-reported, optional, used only within your own weekly reflection.
Notification preferenceUsed only to schedule a local on-device reminder; this never leaves your device.

Data we do not collect

  • We do not access your location, camera, photos, contacts, or microphone.
  • We do not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • We do not share, sell, or rent your data to any third party.

Where your data is stored

Your account and app data are stored in a Supabase project (Supabase is our database and authentication provider). Access to your data is restricted so that only you — via your authenticated account — can read or write your own rows.

How long we keep your data

We retain your account and data for as long as your account exists. You can request deletion at any time by contacting us at the email above, and we will delete your account and associated data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data. To exercise any of these rights, email us and we'll action your request.

Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children and is not intended for use by anyone under 16.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.

Summary: this is a small, personal app used by invited testers. We collect only what's needed to run your account and store your own reflections — nothing is sold, shared, or used for advertising.
